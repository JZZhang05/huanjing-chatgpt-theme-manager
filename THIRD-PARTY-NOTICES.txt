FFmpeg is NOT included in this installer. On first new-wallpaper conversion,
Ensure-ImportTools.ps1 downloads the unmodified Windows binary from the pinned
imageio-ffmpeg 0.6.0 PyPI wheel and verifies the full archive SHA-256 before
extraction. Download details and source links are in tools/ffmpeg-download.json.
That downloaded binary is GPLv3; it is separately downloaded by the end user.

FFmpeg build scripts: https://github.com/imageio/imageio-ffmpeg/tree/v0.6.0/binaries
